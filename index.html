SHiVAi-rs

Markers of cerebral small vessel disease, entirely in your browser: perivascular spaces (PVS) from a T1-weighted image, and white matter hyperintensities (WMH) and lacunes from a T1 with a FLAIR of the same session. No image leaves your computer.

This is a Rust port of SHiVAi's default pipeline: the SHiVA brain mask (two passes), 1 mm conforming, quickshear defacing, a 160 × 214 × 176 crop on the brain and 99th-percentile normalisation, then each marker as the mean of its model's folds, thresholded into clusters. The pipeline runs as WebAssembly; the networks run on WebGPU. The FLAIR is registered rigidly to the T1 with niimath (-allineate -warp shr -cost nmi) where SHiVAi uses ANTs. Cerebral microbleeds (SWI) are not offered.

Results are on the cropped 1 mm T1 grid. Against SHiVAi itself on the example: PVS Dice 0.999, WMH 0.97, lacunes 1.0.

Not for clinical use. Code: AGPL-3.0-or-later (derived from SHiVAi, © Fealinx). Models: SHiVA segmentation models © Fealinx (Philippe Boutinaud et al.), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, converted to .shiva and downloaded once from Hugging Face. Visualization: NiiVue; DICOM import: dcm2niix.

Cite: Boutinaud et al., Front Neuroinform 2021;15:641600 (PVS); Tsuchida et al., Hum Brain Mapp 2024;45:e26548 (WMH). Requires a browser with WebGPU and a GPU.
